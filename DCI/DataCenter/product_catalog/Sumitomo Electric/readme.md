# Sumitomo Electric - Data Center Solutions

Source company: Sumitomo Electric Lightwave (SEL), a Sumitomo Electric Group company.

The folder structure follows SEL's official Data Center Solutions taxonomy:
- Cable Assemblies / SWK™ Series
- Cassettes & Interconnect Panels
- Entrance Frames
- Fiber Panels & Shelves
- Other Enclosures
- Wall Mount Enclosures
- Ribbon Breakout & Fanout Kits
