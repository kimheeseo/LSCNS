## Description source

The catalog description follows US Conec's official MT-ferrule information. MT means **Mechanical Transfer**. The ferrule is a precision, multi-fiber PPS thermoplastic component that uses two stainless-steel guide pins and precision-molded alignment holes. The family supports up to 72 holes for 125 µm optical fiber with 0.25 mm fiber pitch. US Conec identifies MTP®/MPO assemblies and custom passive/active optical coupling packages as applications.

US Conec also distinguishes Standard and MT Elite® performance grades. Its published catalog states maximum insertion-loss values of 0.35 dB for single-mode MT Elite® and multimode MT Elite® products, while standard-loss multimode MT ferrules are specified at 0.6 dB maximum when tested at 850 nm on OM2/OM3/OM4 fiber.
